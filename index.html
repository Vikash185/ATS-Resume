      How the scoring works

      Formatting is checked first. If a resume can't be parsed reliably, an ATS score would be meaningless — so the engine stops and tells you exactly what to fix.

      01 · Gatekeeper

      Formatting must pass

      • At least 100 characters of real, selectable text (rejects scanned images)
      • A valid email or phone number
      • At least 2 of: Experience, Education, Skills, Projects

      02 · ATS score (100 points)

      Six transparent factors

      Section structure25
      Keywords & skills / JD match20
      Quantified impact15
      Action-oriented language15
      Length & readability15
      Contact completeness10
